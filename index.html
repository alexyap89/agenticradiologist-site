01 · about

Two jobs, one skill: reading evidence under uncertainty.

By day I’m a consultant radiologist: musculoskeletal and body imaging, dual-energy CT, and the hospital’s 3D Imaging Lab, where we turn scans into surgical plans, printed models, and quantitative bone characterisation. My workday is a long series of structured evidence — pixels, reports, and the probabilities behind each one.

By night I build local-first agentic AI: small fleets of agents that live on hardware I own, do useful work in my inbox and my research, and never phone home. It started as a way to keep my hands in code. It has become a genuine position — that the interesting future of medical AI is local, inspectable, and owned by the people who use it.

This site is where the two careers overlap. No product, no agency, no launch window — just field notes from a radiologist who happens to run his own models.
